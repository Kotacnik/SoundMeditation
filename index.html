Frequently asked questions

How does the free trial work?

The 7-day trial unlocks every compilation and all editing features. It is free: there is no payment and no automatic charge. When it ends, Meditation, Sleep well and Evening magic stay free, and you can unlock everything with a single one-time purchase. There is no subscription.

How do I restore my purchase?

iPhone and iPad: tap the ··· button above the list, choose Unlock Sound Meditation… and then Restore purchases.
Mac: open Sound Meditation › Settings (⌘,) and click Restore purchases.
Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used for the purchase.

Do I have to buy it again on my other devices?

No. One purchase unlocks Sound Meditation on all your iPhones, iPads and Macs signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I create my own compilation?

Tap + (on the Mac: File › New Compilation or ⌘N), give it a name and add the sounds you like. Each sound has its own volume. You can also duplicate an existing compilation and change it.

I changed a built-in compilation. Can I get the original back?

Yes. Open the compilation’s editor and choose Reset to original, or restore all built-in compilations from the ··· menu (on the Mac: in Settings).

Does the sound keep playing when the screen is locked?

Yes. Sound Meditation plays in the background, and you can pause, skip between compilations and move the timer from the lock screen or with your headphones. After a phone call, playback resumes on its own.

How does the timer work?

Tap the timer button in the player and choose a duration. When the time is up, the sound fades out softly over 30 seconds. The app remembers your last setting.
