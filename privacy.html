Privacy Policy

Sound Meditation & Relaxation · effective 9 November 2026

Support

In short

Sound Meditation does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What the app stores

Your compilations, sound volumes and settings (such as the timer length) are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. If you use iCloud Backup or a Mac backup, this data is included in your backup by Apple, under Apple’s privacy policy.

Purchases

The free trial and the one-time unlock are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details. The app only checks with the App Store, on your device, whether the trial or the unlock is active.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code, and it does not connect to any server of ours.

Children

Because the app collects no personal data, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

If you have any questions about privacy, write to kotacnik@hotmail.com.
